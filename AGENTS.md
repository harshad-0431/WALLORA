# Project Rules: Content, Design & Monetization Guidelines

These rules are strictly enforced across all architecture, asset creation, UI/UX design, and monetization implementations:

1. **Original & Licensed Assets**:
   - Use only original, properly licensed, or legally permitted wallpapers, artwork, and media.
   - Never use copyrighted content without explicit permission.

2. **Original Design & Branding**:
   - Create unique, original UI/UX, layouts, and branding.
   - Do not clone or copy another app's complete design, structure, or assets.

3. **User-Centric & Non-Intrusive Monetization**:
   - Keep advertisements limited, contextual, and non-intrusive.
   - Never interrupt core user workflows or every action with interstitial ads.
   - The app must remain **fully functional and accessible** even when advertisements are disabled, blocked, or fail to load.

4. **Integrity & Compliance**:
   - Strictly follow advertising network policies (e.g., Google AdMob/AdSense) and App Store / Play Store guidelines.
   - Never incentivize ad clicks or generate invalid ad impressions.
   - Never use or support fake downloads, fabricated reviews, or manipulated rankings.

5. **Focus on Quality & Retention**:
   - Prioritize user experience, performance, privacy, and genuine user value over ad impression counts.
   - Build for sustainable retention and user satisfaction rather than relying on volume metrics alone.
